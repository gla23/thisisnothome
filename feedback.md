# Landscape

In order to really internalise whole chapters you need two things:

1. Some kind of storage method
2. Some kind of review schedule

There are a huge number of ways to do both of these things, and each method will have pros and cons for different people.

Storing methods:

- Rote memory: Your brain can store things naturally by repetition, especially when spoken aloud
- Memory palace: Attach content to locations in a journey
- Kinesthetic memory: Physical movements make recall as simple as muscle memory
- Mnemonic techniques: Encoding content with the major sytem, peg lists, etc enable "digging up" memories and error checking
- Visual memory: Engage your creativity with painting, drawing, or even...
- **Chapter maps**: This is what I have invented and am sharing here in case others find it useful

Review methods:

- Ad hoc review whenever you notice something is not as fresh as it was
- Make yourself a schedule e.g. go over chapters on decreasing regularity
- Review when you "walk past" the text if it's anchored to a location you visit or travel through
- Digital spaced repetition e.g. BibleMemory app

The chapter map method that this page exaplains works with any review method.
